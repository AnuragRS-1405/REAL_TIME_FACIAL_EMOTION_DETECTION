Analytical Conclusion

CNN is expected to outperform ANN for image-based emotion recognition because convolutional layers preserve spatial information and learn local facial features—edges, eye shape, eyebrow position, mouth curvature— through hierarchical convolution operations.

ANN flattens the image into a 1D vector, discarding spatial relationships between pixels. While this can still learn colour/intensity patterns, it lacks the inductive bias needed for vision tasks and thus requires more parameters to achieve comparable accuracy.

The CNN, with batch normalisation, dropout, and global average pooling, provides better generalisation across all 7 emotion classes and a more compact, deployable architecture.
