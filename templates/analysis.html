🔬 Key Findings

🧠

CNN vs ANN

CNN outperforms ANN because convolutional layers preserve spatial structure, learning local edge, texture, and expression patterns directly from pixels.

⚖️

Class Imbalance

Happy has significantly more images than Disgust. Class weights and data augmentation help balance training across all 7 emotion classes.

📉

Overfitting Control

Dropout, BatchNormalization, L2 regularization, and EarlyStopping with best-weight restoration prevent overfitting on both models.

🎯

Preprocessing Consistency

Training and inference use identical preprocessing: grayscale, 64×64 resize, and [0,1] normalization to ensure reliable real-world predictions.
